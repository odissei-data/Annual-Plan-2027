Financial plan 2027

One square is about €50,000, rounded per task.

In 2027, ODISSEI's work is funded from several sources

  • The ODISSEI core budget, including membership fees
  • The SSHOC-NL and Macroscope projects
  • The PDI-SSH projects D3I, PANL, POPNET, SANE, FBB, FIP and EUR Coordination
  • MUW and CD2
  • The LSRI upgrade Deepfield
  • The EU projects AI4Social and CDIF4EOSC
